# Image cards for practice areas

The home-page practice section uses eight primary image cards on a warm off-white background. The two existing Energy/Natural Resources and Tax services remain accessible in a native, keyboard-operable “Browse all areas” disclosure. Corporate disputes are covered by the existing Litigation/Arbitration service rather than a duplicate card.

The six existing service slugs remain unchanged. Four service pages have been added for Contracts/Agreements, Real Estate/Construction, Family/Personal Status and Property/Rental Disputes. These use the existing service-page template, metadata, structured data and dynamic sitemap. Descriptions explain the scope of a consultation without asserting legal rules or promising results.

All card headings and descriptions are HTML text. Images are decorative illustrations, not representations of the firm's premises or employees. Each card links to its service page. Images use local WebP files, declared dimensions, lazy loading and asynchronous decoding. Three columns reduce to two on tablets and one on phones. Focus indicators, native disclosure behavior and reduced-motion support are retained.

This change is limited to practice areas, related service content, and their image assets. Team management, news, admin authentication and CMS data are unaffected.

Image provenance and prompts: `docs/practice-image-prompts.md`.
